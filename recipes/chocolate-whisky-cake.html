Decadent Chocolate Whisky Cake

Description

This rich and mousse-like cake has just the right amount of kick from the whisky with a smooth mouth feel.

It may sound like a lot of chocolate and butter, because it is!

Ingredients

Cake

  • 200g quality dark chocolate, broken up
  • 250g softened butter
  • 6 eggs, separated
  • 80g cake flour
  • 1/2 cup castor sugar
  • 1/2 cup whisky
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar

Icing

  • 200g milk chocolate
  • 125ml cream

Steps

Cake

  1. Butter and line a 26 cm springform tin with baking paper.
  2. Melt chocolate and water together in a double-boiler (or a pot over a pan of simmering water).
  3. Beat the chocolate and water mixture slowly, adding softened butter bit by bit until a smooth cream forms.
  4. In a separate bowl, beat 6 egg yolks with castor sugar until light.
  5. Beat in the chocolate mixture by hand and fold in the flour & whisky.
  6. Beat the egg whites with a pinch of cream of tartar until stiff peaks form, then fold into the chocolate mixture lightly, one third at a time.
  7. Bake 25–35 minutes at 160°C. It will begin to shrink from the sides when done. Leave to rest in the pan for at least ten minutes before turning out.

Icing

  1. Melt milk chocolate with 125 ml cream.
  2. Cool until a pourable ganache consistency.
  3. Pour over the cake and allow to spread down the sides (be careful if spreading with a knife as the cake is quite airy and delicate).
  4. Serve with vanilla ice cream or fresh pear slices to complement the whisky.
