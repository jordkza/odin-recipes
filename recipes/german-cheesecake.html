The Best Cheesecake

Description

This deceptively light cheesecake gets its airy, fluffy texture from separating the eggs and carefully folding beaten egg whites into the cream cheese base.

You can add berries of your choice to the mixture (remember to bring them to room temperature first to minimize cracking and avoid affecting the rise).

Ingredients

Pastry

  • 150g cake flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 65g sugar
  • 1 whole egg
  • 65g butter
  • 1 tbsp extra flour for edges
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Filling

  • 750g smooth cottage cheese
  • 150g sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla essence
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest)
  • 250ml cream
  • 65g Maizena / corn starch

Steps

Pastry

  1. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, vanilla & egg. Add diced butter and mix thoroughly until combined.
  2. Press two thirds of pastry into the base of a 26 cm springform tin.
  3. Mix remaining third with extra flour, roll into a long sausage and place around the edge of the pastry. Press into the sides to a height of 2 cm to form a crust.
  4. Pierce base all over with a fork and bake for 15 minutes at 200°C.

Filling

  1. Combine cream cheese, sugar, vanilla, egg yolks, lemon juice & zest and cream (with corn starch stirred into the cream to break up lumps beforehand).
  2. Add the cream and corn starch mixture to the cream cheese mixture.
  3. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks and fold into mixture until just combined.
  4. Pour onto base and spread evenly.
  5. Bake 90 minutes at 140–150°C at the bottom of the oven, then a further 15 minutes at 100°C.
  6. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, loosen edges with a knife. Cool in the tin before removing the sides.
