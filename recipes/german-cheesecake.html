The Best Cheesecake

Description

This deceptively light cheesecake gets its airy, fluffy texture from separating the eggs and carefully folding beaten egg whites into the cream cheese base.

You can add berries of your choice to the mixture (remember to bring them to room temperature first to minimize cracking and avoid affecting the rise).

Ingredients

    Pastry

  • 150g cake flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 65g sugar
  • 1 whole egg
  • 65g butter
  • 1 tablespoon extra flour for edges
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

    • Filling

  • 750g smooth cottage cheese
  • 150g sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla essence
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest)
  • 250ml cream
  • 65g Maizena/corn starch

Steps

    Pastry

  1. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, vanilla & egg. Add diced butter & mix thoroughly until combined.
  2. Press two thirds of pastry into base of a 26cm springform tin.
  3. Mix remaining third with extra flour, roll into long sausage & place round edge of pastry. Press into the sides to height of 2cm to form a crust.
  4. Pierce base all over with a fork & bake for 15 minutes at 200°.

    5. Filling

  5. Combine cream cheese, sugar, vanilla, egg yolks, lemon juice & zest and cream (with corn starch stirred into the cream to break up lumps beforehand).
  6. Add the cream and corn starch mixture to the cream cheese mixture.
  7. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks & fold into mixture until just combined.
  8. Pour onto base and spread evenly.
  9. Bake 90 minutes at 140-150°C at bottom of oven. Then bake a further 15 minutes or so at 100°C.
  10. As soon as cake comes out of oven, loosen edges with a knife. Cool in tin before removing the sides.
