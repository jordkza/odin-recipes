Golden Spiced Apple Cake

Description

Warm flavours of cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg pair with a butterscotch glaze for this teatime favourite.

For a less sweet cake you can leave out the glaze.

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1½ cups cake flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar (treacle)
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp rum or brandy (optional) + 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 cup chopped apples
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Butterscotch Glaze

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar (treacle)
  • 1/3 cup cream or evaporated milk
  • 2 cups sifted icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla essence or 1 tsp rum

Steps

Cake

  1. Butter a large baking tin.
  2. Combine the cake flour, brown sugar, bicarb, spices and salt (and the rum and vanilla mixture if using) and mix well.
  3. Stir in the chopped apples and walnuts.
  4. Bake 40–45 minutes at 180°C.

Glaze

  1. Heat butter, brown sugar and cream/milk in a double boiler until smooth, then cool.
  2. Gradually add sifted icing sugar and vanilla/rum until pourable or spreadable.
  3. Pour over the cake and spread down the sides.
  4. Serve with spiced chai tea or any beverage that complements the warm, nutty flavours.
