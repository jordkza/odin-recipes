Golden Spiced Apple Cake

Description

Warm flavours of cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg pair with a butterscotch glaze for this teatime favourite.

For a less sweet cake you can leave out the glaze.

Ingredients

    Cake

  • 1 & a half cups cake flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar (treacle)
  • 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons rum or brandy (optional), mixed with 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • 1 cup chopped apples
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

    • Butterscotch glaze

  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar (treacle)
  • 1/3 cup cream or evaporated milk
  • 2 cups sifted icing sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla essence or 1 teaspoon rum

Steps

    Cake

  1. Butter a large baking tin.
  2. Combine the cake flour, cup of brown sugar, bicarb, spices and salt (and the rum and vanilla mixture if using) and mix well.
  3. Stir in the chopped apples and walnuts.
  4. Bake 40-45 minutes at 180°C.

    5. Glaze

  5. Heat butter, brown sugar and cream/milk in double boiler until smooth, then cool.
  6. Gradually add in sifted icing sugar and vanilla/rum until pourable or spreadable (depending whether you prefer icing or a glaze).
  7. Pour over cake and spread down sides.
  8. Serve with spiced chai tea or any other beverage that will compliment the warm and nutty flavours.
